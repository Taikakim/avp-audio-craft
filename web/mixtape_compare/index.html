What the three mixes are
The same 16 shortlist clips in the same order and timing, each extended 3x, kick-grid aligned. Only the transitions differ.
A, bass hand-over: a 150 Hz band split; the kick and bass hand over once the kick grids stop clashing, the rest is swept.
B, stem staging: synths cross over 2 bars, then drums over 4 bars, then kick and bass last (plain crossfades of the stems).
C, inpainted: the middle 4 bars of the drum crossfade and 2 bars of the synth crossfade are regenerated by the model; kick and bass from the stems.
