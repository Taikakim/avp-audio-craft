## What the reader sees

One rank slider, 8 stops: 16, 32, 64, 128, 256, 512, 1024, 1536 (default 128), and a "DoRA" on/off button (default off).
Three parts, stacked:

1. **The bottleneck.** A 1536-wide layer drawn as input bar -> narrow middle of width r -> output bar. A reads from the
   input (r × 1536), B writes the correction (1536 × r). The middle's drawn width follows r (log scale, so 16 is still
   visible). Readout: "Adapter: r × (1536 + 1536) = <n> numbers, <p>% of the full matrix's 2,359,296." With DoRA on, add
   "+ 1,536 magnitudes (one per row)".
2. **How much of a full fine-tune's change fits.** A chart: x = rank (log scale, 16 to 1536), y = energy kept (0 to 100%).
   The five measured points as filled dots, the full matrix at 1536 as a hollow dot labelled "100% by definition", a dashed
   line between neighbouring points (interpolated, linear in log2 rank), and a marker at the chosen rank. Readout:
   "<e>% of the change kept" plus its kind: "measured", "interpolated between r<a> and r<b>", or "the full matrix".
   A short line under it: "Holding 90% of one matrix's change took a median rank of 747 to 1,110."
3. **A small example.** A 12 × 12 toy weight change, drawn as three heatmaps: "Full change", "Kept at rank k" and
   "Missing". k is the toy rank that matches the slider: k = max(1, round(12 r / 1536)), so r128 -> 1, r512 -> 4,
   r1024 -> 8, r1536 -> 12; the readout says "toy rank k of 12 (the toy is 1/128 of the real width)", and for r below 128
   adds "the toy is too small to show ranks below 128, so it shows rank 1" (otherwise the toy's 25.7% would contradict the
   chart's 4.5% at r16). The toy's singular
   values are set so that its energy kept at k = 1, 4, 8, 12 equals the measured curve at r128, r512, r1024, 1536.
   With DoRA on, 12 small dials appear beside the "Kept" heatmap, one per row, showing each row's length: a needle for the
   LoRA-truncated row and a tick for the target row (the fully fine-tuned weights); DoRA turns each needle onto its tick
   (~400 ms). Readout "#vz-rank-rows": off: "Row lengths are off by <x>% on average at toy rank k"; on: "Row lengths match
   exactly: DoRA sets each row's length with its own number. The directions still come from the rank-k part."
Moving the slider eases the heatmaps and the marker to their new state (~300 ms); the loop goes idle when everything has
arrived and resumes on any change.

Node tests (`node --test site/help/viz/tests/`), each a real check:
1. `energyAt` returns the MEASURED values with kind 'measured' at 16, 64, 128, 512, 1024; 1 and 'full' at 1536; at 32 the
   kind is 'interpolated' with `between` [16, 64] and e = (0.045 + 0.148) / 2 (exactly halfway in log2); at 256 between
   [128, 512]; values increase along RANKS; throws at 8 and 2048.
2. `adapterParams(128)` = 393,216; `fullParams()` = 2,359,296.
3. `toyRank` over RANKS gives [1, 1, 1, 1, 2, 4, 8, 12].
4. `keptEnergy(makeToy(), k)` equals energyAt(128/512/1024/1536).e at k = 1/4/8/12 within 1e-12; the shares are
   non-increasing (s sorted largest first).
5. `orthogonal(12, g)`: U^T U is the identity within 1e-10.
6. `truncate(toy, 12)` equals `dW` within 1e-10; `frobenius(truncate(toy, k))^2 / frobenius(dW)^2` equals `keptEnergy(toy, k)`
   within 1e-10 for every k.
7. `rowLengths(toy, k).meanRelErr` is > 0 for k < 12 and < 1e-10 at k = 12; it is smaller at k = 8 than at k = 1.
8. Determinism: `makeToy(5)` twice is identical; `makeToy(6)` differs.

## Track B: the module (`site/help/viz/lora-rank.js`)

Follows `qkv-line.js` in style: `mount(container, params, help) -> { destroy() }`, `injectStyle`, `applyColors`, `loop()`
(pauses out of view; idles when arrived; with `help.reducedMotion` every change jumps to its end state and nothing moves by
itself; the Step button kit adds may simply do nothing visible), `realNote()` required. Imports only from `./lib/`. Rules from
`lib/kit.js`: never name a colour in text (heatmaps use one diverging scale from `help.colors`: negative -> `bad`, zero ->
`bg`, positive -> `accent`, with a small "−  0  +" key drawn beside them; dial needle vs tick are told apart by shape and
the key text "needle = with this adapter, tick = target"); `help.openTerm` only for ids in the rank entry's text (fine not to
call it). The heatmap colour scale is fixed across all three heatmaps and all ranks (max |value| of the full change).

DOM contract (track C tests against it; do not rename):
- root `div.vz-rank` with `data-rank` (the number), `data-dora` (`on`|`off`), `data-toyrank` (k).
- `input#vz-rank-r[type=range]`, min 0, max 7, step 1 (index into RANKS), `<label for>` "Rank", `aria-valuetext`
  "rank <r>"; `output#vz-rank-r-out` shows r.
- `button` with text "DoRA" and `aria-pressed`.
- `svg.vz-bottleneck` containing `rect.middle` whose `data-r` is the rank.
- `svg.vz-energy` containing exactly 5 `circle.measured`, one `circle.full`, one `path.interp`, one `circle.marker` (with
  `data-e` = energyAt(r).e, full precision).
- three `svg.vz-heat` with classes `full`, `kept`, `missing`, each with exactly 144 `rect.cell`; each cell carries `data-v`
  (its value, full precision) once arrived.
- `g.dials` (inside or beside the kept heatmap) with exactly 12 `g.dial`, hidden (`visibility="hidden"` or the `hidden`
  attribute on its container) while DoRA is off.
- readouts `#vz-rank-params`, `#vz-rank-energy` (percentage), `#vz-rank-kind` ("measured" / "interpolated between r<a> and
  r<b>" / "the full matrix"), `#vz-rank-toy`, `#vz-rank-rows`.
- the "What's real here" note (via `realNote`).
Defaults: rank 128, DoRA off. `params` may override: `{rank: <one of RANKS>, dora: true|false}`; invalid values fall back.
Works at 360 px wide: no horizontal overflow; the three heatmaps may wrap or shrink but stay at least 90 px wide each; text at
least 11 px rendered.

"What's real here" content:
- Computed live: the parameter counts; the toy change and its rank-k part, built from the toy's own singular vectors; the row
  lengths with and without DoRA's magnitudes.
- From our own runs: the energy a uniform rank keeps of a full fine-tune of the medium model (229 weight matrices, width 1,536):
  4.5% at rank 16, 14.8% at 64, 25.7% at 128, 65% at 512, 89.5% at 1,024, the same within 0.2 points across all 10 of our full
  fine-tunes (measured 2026-07-24). Holding 90% of one matrix's change took a median rank of 747 (attention output) to 1,110
  (MLP).
- Illustration only: values between the measured ranks are interpolated on a log scale; the toy is 12 wide and its spectrum is
  shaped to match the measured curve at four ranks; we have no measured curve for DoRA, and in the toy DoRA only fixes row
  lengths (a trained DoRA also learns its directions with them).

For local testing before track A lands, Track B may write a stub `lib/lora.js` that follows the contract, but must NOT commit
it (only `lora-rank.js` is committed).
