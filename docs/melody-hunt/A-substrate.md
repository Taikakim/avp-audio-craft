# Melody hunt, track A: is melody in the substrate at all? Where, when, what formed it

Evidence dossier (not reader prose). Compiled 2026-11-03, read-only, CPU only. Scope: what SAME's latent carries, the melody subspace, recoverability by noise level, where in the DiT melody commits, what fine-tuning does to melody, and the corpus side. Guidance heads, conditioners and meters belong to other tracks and appear only where this track depends on them.

Entry format: id, date, owner, question, method, number, n, meter (+ positive control?), MEASURED / HEARD, source. `UNSOURCED` = I could not trace it. Paths are relative to /home/kim/Projects/SAO unless absolute. Line numbers are as of 2026-11-03.

Abbreviations: C = CONTINUITY, W = WINTERMUTE, F = THE-FINN, G = GHOST-NOTE, AG = ANTIGRAVITY. EXP = EXPERIMENTS.md. cos12 = cosine of the 12-d folded chroma. "demeaned" = corpus-mean 12-d profile subtracted per band before cosine.

## 6. Possible blind spots (my speculation, labelled)

- SPECULATION: nearly every "is melody in z" probe uses SYNTHETIC notes (fluidsynth) or MuScriptor skyline labels. The one real-audio, human-labelled-melody test in the repo is the No Doubt in-mix floor (S15). A small handful of goa tracks with DAW-truth MIDI (the Two Suns attempt, S22) is the clean experiment; it was parked.
- SPECULATION: the melody basis is a 15-d LINEAR subspace of z chosen to be melody-variant on synthetic intervals. If goa melody is mostly "which pitch classes recur at the pedal" (S04), the informative part may live in slow, repeated structure that a per-frame subspace loss cannot weight; the static-vs-temporal probe (S26) argues against fingerprint contamination but not against "wrong kind of structure".
- SPECULATION: the training objective shows "melody share ~ 1/11 of variance" (S03) but the dataset's prompts carry no melody information (prompts like "earth chakra, 1996, 123"; `REPORT-2026-07-22.md` so-what 1). The model has no text handle for the thing we are asking it to produce; some of what is called a substrate gap may be a caption gap. Caption-tag experiments are the other tracks' business but the substrate answer depends on them.
- SPECULATION: CFG in the post-trained `medium` is meant to be 1 (CLAUDE.md), yet S05's cfg 7 -> 16 comparison includes base_ptm. If base_ptm was rendered at cfg 7/16 it is off its native operating point; the drop in hmr may be partly an off-config artifact.
- SPECULATION: SDEdit washes attacks, collapsing MuScriptor coverage to 0-0.47 (S06 side-finding). Any transcription-based melody metric on a2a outputs is partly a transcribability meter.
- SPECULATION: EMA / per-microbatch EMA horizon could affect which checkpoint "has melody", e.g. Kim's ear verdicts across epochs (ep11 vs ep19 differences) may be partly EMA-warm-up. Not checked here; see the `sa3-training` skill.
- SPECULATION: no experiment asks whether the SAME DECODER can render an arbitrary melody latent (encode MIDI-render -> decode -> transcribe round-trip with goa timbres). The codec fidelity for melody (not just distance) is only implied by S14/S15 (displacement) and the Gate G0 drum result (`DISCOVERIES.md:186`).
- SPECULATION: melody over time scale: the R2(t) and D10 work is about frame-level updates; goa melodies are 1-3-note cells (S04), but D16 found a pitch head follows a triangle wave only at 10 s periods (`EXPERIMENTS.md:~723`). The temporal scale at which melody is "decided" in the DiT is never measured.
