# Track B dossier: steering pitch at inference with LatCH guidance heads (chroma and f0)

Compiled 2026-11-03 by a read-only research pass. Evidence dossier, not prose for readers.
Conventions: "W" = WINTERMUTE, "C" = CONTINUITY, "AG" = ANTIGRAVITY, "G" = GHOST-NOTE. MEASURED = a number from a meter; HEARD = a human listened and said something.
"Meter" = the instrument that produced the number. "PC" = positive control. Source paths are relative to /home/kim/Projects/SAO unless absolute; run data lives under `/run/media/kim/Mantu/{sa3_control_runs,latch_sweep}/__theme/` and `/home/kim/evals_aac/control_runs/`.
All pages named below are in `/home/kim/evals_aac/` and are served at `https://aavepyora.online/files/evals/<name>.html` (URL base from KIM-TASKLIST.md:13; not re-fetched by me).

Short answer to the core question, with the hedges in sections 4-6: guidance gives **harmonic colour and a slow register direction**, not notes. A 384-d chroma head (retrained, E13) moves the output chroma toward a requested chord and follows transposed chords at gain 2048 (42/42 cells). The f0 lead head moves pitch direction over a whole 20 s clip (+22.1 semitones rising-minus-falling at gain 8192, 12/12) but follows nothing faster than a 10 s triangle wave. Note-level melody turning with guidance was tested once (2026-07-22, production head, 0/12) and has not been re-run with any head built since.

## 6) Possible blind spots (my speculation, labelled)

- *Speculation:* Guidance targets are scored at the same representation they are trained on. A different meter (key detection, MuScriptor skyline) could show the SAME chroma Delta is a texture/"tint" effect that does not change what a transcriber hears; B-08 found exactly this (chroma Delta small, transcription adoption near null) once.
- *Speculation:* Nobody has asked what happens to chroma guidance on the DoRA-finetuned goa model with a real melodic prompt versus the raw base. E21b/c, E22 use the goa finetune; E13 uses raw medium-base; the numbers differ (+0.152 vs +0.080) and nobody has crossed the two.
- *Speculation:* The f0 heads are scored against a Melodia curve on the mix; the head's own training target was Melodia on the separated `other` stem. A render in which the lead gets louder satisfies both without a new melody. The fair test is melody-transcription accuracy of notes at the requested pitches (MuScriptor), not slope.
- *Speculation:* Chroma guidance and the f0 head may be complementary (chroma = what notes, f0 = which register/direction) but they have never been composed; LATCH_SLOTS = 3 allows it.
- *Speculation:* The 06-25 "pitch class dominant at 1536-2048" claim was on an all-C vs all-F# contrast, a static single-key target. All later progression/key-lift results are weaker (Delta ~+0.1-0.17 at 2048) and the "Am -> F -> C -> G" targets have never been scored for chord ORDER correctness (only mid-band cos to the target time series).
- *Speculation:* The gain-ceiling notion differs by metric: gate ceiling 3072-8192, hit peak maybe 4096, cos still rising at 32768, per-head. A "usable gain" quoted without naming which of those is easy to over-read.
- *Speculation:* The "gain-0 baseline trap" and the `expandable_segments` fault may have affected the 07-19 chroma_steer pilot (it ran earlier than the flag-setting scripts, so possibly not) and the older model-matrix corpus (MASTER notes this unexplained).
- *Speculation:* Guidance acts on a 10.77 Hz latent grid; a 16th note at 143 BPM is ~9.5 Hz, close to Nyquist (5.4 Hz). D16's ceiling (10 s) and the 0/12 result might partly be a grid/resolution effect rather than head weakness; a test: same shape at lower pitch rate with a stronger head.
