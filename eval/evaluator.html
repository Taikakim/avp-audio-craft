How this works

Length dropdown
Picks how long the clips are -- the standard 20s grid, or a native length up to ~380s. Changing it loads a whole new pair of clips at that length, not the same pair replayed longer -- that's expected, not a glitch.
The circle in the middle
Tap it to switch which of the two tracks (A or B) you're currently hearing -- both play from the exact same moment, so switching is a fair blind comparison, never a restart. Whichever track is currently playing is also the one the rate row below activates for -- so switch first, then rate what you just heard. Drag sideways on it to scrub both tracks to a new position at once.
A / No difference / B buttons
Answer the single question shown above them (which track sounds clearer, with its elements standing out distinctly instead of blurring into mush, and with detail that feels meaningful rather than aimless noise) by picking the one you preferred, or the middle button if you truly can't tell them apart. This is a comparison between the two tracks -- separate from the 0-5 rating below, which scores each track on its own.

What happens when I'm done
Once you've answered the A/B question and rated both A and B 0-5, a brand new pair loads automatically -- there's no "next" button because you don't need one.
