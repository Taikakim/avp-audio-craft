How to read the automatic metrics — and how not to. The two Audiobox scores do not carry equal weight here. Production Quality (PQ) tracks listening selection well — on earlier analyses of the evaluator-GUI data it accounted for roughly 70% of which arms were actually picked, so it is a genuine predictor and is presented as one. Content Enjoyment (CE) does not, on this set: it scores the arm judged weakest by ear highest of all (CE 6.92). Do not read the CE column as a ranking on this page. Separately, the DSP screen behind these numbers carries no beat-steadiness measure — and steadiness was the actual discriminator in the audition. No conclusion about steadiness can be drawn from anything shown here; that one still needs the ear.
