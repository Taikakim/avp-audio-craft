FusionOpt

A composed PyTorch optimiser that fuses seven published methods — Muon, NorMuon, Schedule-Free, MONA, KL-Shampoo, ScheduleFree+ — plus a TimeConditioningCache for diffusion inference. The components are well-cited; the composition is the novel part.

It's the optimiser that trains the control heads and adapters in this project. Released to the public domain (CC0 1.0) — one of the few pieces here clean enough to hand off whole. github.com/Taikakim/fusion-optimiser

§ What it fuses

componentmechanism
Muonspectral orthogonalisation of the update (Newton–Schulz)
NorMuonper-neuron row scaling on top of the orthogonalised step
Schedule-FreePolyak/averaging — no LR schedule to tune
MONAcurvature-aware momentum
KL-ShampooKronecker-factored preconditioner
ScheduleFree+Polyak step-size selection
TimeConditioningCachecaches adaLN-zero time embeddings → diffusion inference speedup

§ The production recipe

The load-bearing subset is SF-NorMuon — {ns5, normuon, sf} at bf16. Empirically it captures ~95% of the full composition's quality lift at ~50% of the wall-clock cost, on small-to-medium DiT-style models with adaLN-zero conditioning.

optimizer = FusionOpt(
    params=build_fusion_param_groups(model),
    lr=3e-4,
    components={"ns5", "normuon", "sf"},   # SF-NorMuon
    hot_dtype="bf16",                       # NOT fp16 — NS5 overflows
)

For diversity-incentivised training (penalising similarity to a frozen reference) the full composition is warm-started from an SF-NorMuon checkpoint instead.

§ When it helps

✓ yes✗ no / unclear
transformer-style 2D weight matrices (qkv, projections, MLPs ≥ 128×128) pure conv nets — spectral methods are matrix-aware, not for 4-D kernels
adaLN-zero conditioning (gives the time cache its leverage) very small networks (< 1 M params) — overhead may dominate
mid-range scales, ~5 M – 10 B params workloads where per-element adaptivity beats spectral geometry

§ Evidence, not guarantees

The optimiser trains the LatCH guidance heads and the sa3_control adapters that steer Stable Audio 3 in this project — exactly the DiT-style, adaLN-zero targets it's built for. The sharpest single result to date: FusionCC, a run that puts a learned onset-meter inside the training gradient, moved onset-control correlation .584 → .880 (bootstrap CI clear of zero) and broke the sparse-rhythm floor that every other head had hit. That's the meter inside the gradient actually biting.

Research code: treat these numbers as evidence for our workload, not as a guarantee for yours. The repo ships docs/best_practices.md and docs/open_questions.md in that spirit. Requires torch >= 2.2; no other runtime dependencies.
