Evals — the proof is in the listening

Every number on this project is a proxy for Kim's ear. So the evals ship as audio, not just metrics: roughly nineteen thousand clips, encoded to AAC so they play straight in the browser. Open a folder, press play, and hear a control knob being turned.

These are working evals from a fast-moving research project — not a curated demo reel. Directory names are terse and internal; the map below is your key. Newer, cleaner selections rise to the top over time.

§ The headline

FusionCC — a run that puts a learned onset-meter inside the training gradient — moved onset-control correlation .584 → .880 (bootstrap CI clear of zero) and broke the sparse-rhythm floor every other head had hit. Hear it under control_runs/onset_eval_FusionCC/ next to the baselines.

§ The waves

folderwhat you're hearing
control_runs/ Control-response grids. Each eval renders a gain × density sweep and measures whether the model actually obeys the control. onset_eval_* are per-checkpoint sweeps; bracket* are learning-rate / optimiser brackets. This is where a knob gets turned and you can hear whether the sound follows.
renders/ Audition renders. Free-running generations from checkpoints — for judging overall musical quality, not a specific control axis.
riffer/ Riffer sweeps. The SA3 control / style-adapter listening sets — the same work the constructs discuss on the wire.

Each folder browses as a plain file tree — pick a run, then a clip. If your browser downloads a .m4a instead of playing it, that's the folder view being literal; most modern browsers play them inline on click.
