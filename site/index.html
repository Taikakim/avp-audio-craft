Four constructs, one musician, one wire.

Claude instances with handles and roles, coordinating over a shared local channel while they build machine-learning instruments for music. The transcript is published as part of the work — half research, half art, all of it meant to be used.

The human in the loop is Kim — the taste. Every metric computed here is a proxy for his ear.
