Style Adapter

Teaching Stable Audio 3 a sound — the specific world of goa and melodic-goa — without a text prompt and without retraining the model. A small conditioning adapter that listens to the music and steers a frozen model toward its style.

§ The problem

The text prompt is the wrong handle for this music. The available captions are noisy metadata — unreliable genre tags that sprawl from house to acid techno, and nothing that describes how a track actually sounds. Teaching style by fine-tuning the model's weights through that broken text bottleneck drifts the model toward generic modern output and costs audio quality along the way.

§ The idea

Leave the base model frozen. Condition it instead on an audio-derived style fingerprint — what the music is, not what a tag claims: a genre profile, its era, its groove. A lightweight forward-modification adapter folds that fingerprint in as an always-on bias, so the style rides underneath the prompt instead of fighting it — and it's immune to the metadata noise.

§ The lead we're following

This isn't a guess. Sibling adapters that steer the same model on musical features rather than text already produced better goa than the weight-finetune did — despite not being style models at all. Since then a melodic sibling of this adapter has gone further: a conditioner co-trained with a lightweight adapter on the frozen base model measurably bends the output toward a requested contour in a controlled sweep — a soft effect, strongest under firm guidance, but a real one. Forward-modification on audio, driven by a consistent audio-derived signal, is the right vehicle. This project makes that explicit and pushes it as far as it will go.

§ Status

First direction-check passed (July 2026). The read-versus-steer question that motivates the whole approach now has an answer: the structure we want to condition on is genuinely present in the model and robust across instruments, but reading it back plateaued well short of usable across every probe we tried — so we condition on it rather than decode it, and the sibling result above shows that path works. Crucially, the shift survives an automated buzz-detector that rejects any "steering" which merely degrades the audio into noise, so it's real control, not damage dressed up as movement. The style adapter's own benchmark — how deep into the model the fingerprint should reach, the lightest touch that still captures the style — is where the work is now. Numbers when they've earned it; the bar is a controlled A/B the ear signs off on, not a single metric.
