Suggestive but small-n. Net actionable map (to TEST prospectively, not assert): high-R² non-transient → trains fine; low-mid-R² alive → EMA is the lever; very-low-R² non-transient → likely dead pre-training; transient/ onset → screen blind, measure authority directly.
