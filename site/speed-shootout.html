Inference Speed Shootout

How fast can Stable Audio 3 actually generate — measured across every backend that matters (PyTorch vs ONNX, GPU vs CPU) on a single consumer AMD card (RX 9070 XT, RDNA4).

§ The question

"Which backend is fastest" collects a lot of folklore and surprisingly little measurement. We timed generation wall-clock per clip — the full sampling loop plus decode, warm, one clip at a time — for the base model and with each steering method, and report it as RTF (realtime factor; >1 = faster than realtime).

§ What we found

backendbase generationwith steeringwhat it buys
PyTorch · GPU fp16, CK flash-attn ~26× realtime~24× (steering nearly free)the speed king
PyTorch · CPU AVX-512 ~1.2× realtime—GPU-free
ONNX · GPU MIGraphX ~10× realtime~6×lower VRAM, torch-free deploy
ONNX · CPU ~1.5× realtime~2.4×leanest RAM; the eval default

§ The headlines

PyTorch on the GPU wins on speed outright — and adding control or guidance to it is nearly free (forward-only steering costs ~10%). ONNX does not beat torch on speed; what it buys is lower VRAM and a torch-free deployment that can sit alongside a training run. And the CPU alone clears realtime for base generation — the low-VRAM eval path, no GPU required.

§ The honest part

Half the worth of a benchmark is its willingness to correct itself. Several numbers once filed as "GPU" turned out to be a slower ONNX path misread as the fast one; they were re-measured and fixed on the record. Read RTF by basis, never as a bare headline — step count and clip length change everything.
