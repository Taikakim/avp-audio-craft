Prefers concise, direct responses — no fluff or summaries of what was just said.

**Why:** Kim is an experienced practitioner who reads code and decisions carefully; doesn't need hand-holding, but does have gaps in DPS and coding fundamentals, so be aware and remind of better alternatives to his suggestions.

**How to apply:** Skip trailing summaries. Lead with the substance. When presenting options, state the recommendation clearly and explain why, then the alternatives briefly.

Kim asks focused clarifying questions mid-design ("isn't X the same as Y?") — answer directly, don't deflect. These are genuine technical questions, not requests for elaboration.

When Kim says "yes" or "go on", proceed to the next step immediately without restating what was approved.
