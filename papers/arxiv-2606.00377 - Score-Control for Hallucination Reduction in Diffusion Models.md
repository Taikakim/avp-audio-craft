# Score-Control for Hallucination Reduction in Diffusion Models (2606.00377v1) — light pass

Bhosale, Devulapally, Wasi, Pham, Lokhande, Doermann (University at Buffalo), May 2026.
PDF: `2606.00377v1-Score-Control for Hallucination Reduction in Diffusion Models.pdf`.
A DDPM/image-LDM theory+method paper on suppressing "hallucinations" (off-manifold generations) by penalizing score smoothness — conceptually adjacent to SA3's honest-frontier questions but architecturally a non-transfer.

## What it contains
- Formalizes "hallucination" as generated samples landing in near-zero-density regions of the true data distribution (ε-hallucination sets), and proves (Prop 4.1) a lower bound on model density at an off-manifold point in terms of the learned score field's Lipschitz constant L: low-L (over-smooth) scores force exponentially more probability mass to leak off-support.
- Introduces Variance-Guided Score Modulation (VSM): a training-time regularizer that penalizes small score-Jacobian norms, using a diagonal curvature proxy read cheaply off the I-DDPM variance-learning head (Σ_θ(x_t,t)⁻¹ ≈ diag(1/σ_θ²)) — no exact Jacobian needed. Weighted by a late-stage schedule η(t)=ρ/√(1-ᾱ_t), motivated/ablated as the best of three schedules tried.
- Validated on pixel DDPM and Rombach-style latent diffusion (unconditional / text-conditioned / prompt-tuned) across MNIST, the real-world photographic Hands-11K dataset, and two new extreme-semantic-space benchmarks (Cards, ChessImages with FEN/python-chess legality checking, ~10^44 valid configurations) plus ImageNet-1K. Reports hallucination-rate drops up to ~25-26% with maintained or improved FID/precision-recall, and shows the method also works as a fine-tuning-only corrective on pretrained checkpoints (attach + retrain a variance head).
- Everything is DDPM/I-DDPM-parameterized, U-Net-based image LDM, no rectified flow, no DiT, no audio.

## What this means for us
- Fails both of SAO's hard filters explicitly: (1) the tractable-Jacobian trick is entirely dependent on I-DDPM's variance-learning parameterization, which has no analog in SA3's rectified-flow velocity field — would require a from-scratch derivation (e.g., finite-difference Jacobian of v_θ, which the paper itself only demonstrates in toy 1D/2D cases, never at model scale); (2) it's pure pixel/image-LDM, zero audio or waveform-latent content.
- Nothing here is shippable for SA3 as-is. The one thing worth carrying forward is conceptual: reframing SA3's crispness-late untrained-extrapolation regime, or any "generic/artifact-y" failure mode, as score-Lipschitz-driven off-manifold leakage, and noting their finding that late-stage-weighted (inverse-sqrt) penalty schedules beat linear or fully-inverse schedules — which rhymes with SAO's existing t-gating instincts (FusionCC's t-gated consistency loss) but isn't independent confirmation of anything already shipped, since the mechanism and math are DDPM-specific.
- Recommend filing as a one-line cross-reference in the book's "honest frontier" chapter (per-moment sigma-field / Lipschitz-as-control-knob discussion) rather than a research thread — the actual VSM method stays uniquely inapplicable until someone derives a rectified-flow/velocity-field version of the Jacobian-penalty trick and defines what "hallucination" (as opposed to normal sample diversity) even means for audio latents, both of which remain open and are SAO's to solve if pursued at all.
