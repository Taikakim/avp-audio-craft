# Unified Audio Generation and Editing via Joint Condition Modeling and Progressive Training (2606.16435) — light pass

Haocheng Dong, Yuheng Lu, Cheng Gong, Shansong Liu, Xiao-Lei Zhang, Xuelong Li (TeleAI). arXiv:2606.16435v1, June 2026.
PDF: `2606.16435v1-Unified Audio Generation and Editing via Joint Condition Modeling and Progressive Training.pdf`.
A single DiT trained to both generate (TTA) and instruction-edit audio (add/remove/replace/reorder/inpaint/SR) by treating reference audio as concatenated context tokens rather than a channel-expansion input — architecturally off-path for SA3 (mel+vocoder VAE, from-scratch small backbone) but with two reusable training/conditioning ideas.

## What it contains
- **AudioWeave**: hybrid DiT backbone (8 dual-stream MMDiT blocks + 16 single-stream DiT blocks, ~672M params), trained with rectified flow (logistic-normal t-sampling, standard z_t/velocity formulation) on top of MMAudio's VAE (40-ch, 1024x downsampling) + BigVGAN-v2 vocoder — i.e., a mel/vocoder-adjacent pipeline, not a direct-to-waveform latent.
- **Joint condition modeling**: reference audio (when present) is concatenated with target audio along the sequence dimension (not via input-channel expansion, unlike the AUDIT/DiT-Edit lineage). A **factorized position embedding** splits RoPE into a global inter-sequence index and a local intra-sequence index so text/target-audio/reference-audio tokens can jointly attend while remaining positionally distinguishable.
- **Progressive multistage training**: Stage 1 trains TTA only from scratch; Stage 2 mixes TTA and editing data with a **task-specific attention mask** that excludes empty/padding reference-audio tokens from attention for TTA-only samples. Ablation (Table VI) shows the staged approach outperforms naive joint training on nearly all metrics for both tasks (paper's own caveat: except KLPaSST on TTA and FD on editing), and the factorized position embedding outperforms standard RoPE on nearly all metrics (paper's own caveat: except KL divergence on TTA; a clean sweep on editing).
- Six editing tasks are evaluated (adding, removing, replacement, reordering, inpainting, super-resolution) against SAO-Instruct, MMEdit, and a DiT-Edit (channel-expansion) baseline, plus TTA against eight SOTA baselines (AudioLDM2, Tango2, Make-An-Audio2, Stable Audio Open, EzAudio, GenAu, TangoFlux, MeanAudio). Results are competitive/second-best on most metrics, not universally SOTA.

## What this means for us
- **Fails hard filter 2 architecturally**: the VAE-decoder→vocoder pipeline puts this in the mel+vocoder lineage the project's own filters flag as non-transferring, alongside most of its own TTA baselines. No engagement with differential attention, AdaLN, local-additive conditioning, memory tokens, or t-truncation — nothing here speaks to SA3's actual distinguishing mechanisms, so it neither confirms nor corrects anything already known.
- **Not a duplicate of Longform**, but occupies adjacent territory: Longform is training-free (SDEdit re-anchor + slerp crossfade + inpaint-continuation) at sampling time; this is a *trained*, instruction+reference-conditioned editing capability unified with generation in one model. Different paradigm, same functional ground (add/remove/inpaint/SR-like edits) — doesn't argue Longform is deficient, just demonstrates an alternative that SAO hasn't built.
- **Two portable ideas, if SAO ever wants trained (not just sampling-time) instruction-editing on SA3**: (1) concatenate reference-audio latents into the sequence with a global/local-factorized RoPE rather than expanding input channels — a clean conditioning pattern compatible in spirit with SA3's existing local-additive/cross-attention pathways; (2) the empirical catastrophic-forgetting result — naive joint multi-task training measurably underperforms a staged base-then-mix curriculum with task-specific attention masking — is a concrete, citable data point for any future work mixing generation and editing objectives in one SA3-derived model.
- Everything substantive here — the backbone, the VAE/vocoder, the specific editing tasks and their instruction format, the CFG formulation per modality — remains entirely the paper's own construction, with no direct bearing on LatCH, FusionCC, Longform, weight-garden, or DoRA/LoRA adapter work. Low priority; no action needed beyond this note.
