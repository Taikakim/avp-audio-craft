# DirectAudioEdit: Inversion-Free Text-Guided Audio Editing via Diffusion Prediction Contrast (2606.07356) — light pass

Ge, Liu, Zhang, Ge, Zhang, Yu, Zhu, Xiao (Northeastern Univ. China et al.), 2026-06-05.
PDF: `2606.07356v1-DirectAudioEdit_ Inversion-Free Text-Guided Audio Editing via Diffusion Prediction Contrast.pdf`.
DDPM-specific inversion-free audio editing (AudioLDM2/Tango2, mel+vocoder) - fails both SAO hard filters plus the CFG-liveness rule; the paper's own framing says its machinery exists because diffusion SDE paths are curved, unlike RF's straight paths, i.e. its own logic argues FlowEdit (already in the book) is the right tool for SA3, not this.

## What it contains
- Problem: training-free, inversion-free text-guided audio editing on pretrained DDPM-style audio diffusion backbones (AudioLDM2, Tango2), evaluated on event-level (AudioCaps addition/removal/replacement) and music (MedleyMDPrompts) benchmarks.
- Reframes FlowEdit's flow-model "direct path" idea for diffusion models: since diffusion generation paths (SDEs) are curved/uncertain (vs. flow models' near-straight paths), raw source/target velocity- or noise-prediction differences are an unreliable edit direction.
- Mechanism ("Diffusion Prediction Contrast"): at each timestep, re-noise both the fixed source latent Z0_src and the current clean-editing-state estimate Z_t^DP with the SAME sampled noise via the standard DDPM forward kernel (shared-noise re-noising); run one scheduler reverse-update step (Phi_theta) under source and target text conditions; take the difference of the resulting one-step reverse displacements (not raw predictions) as the edit residual, applied to update the clean latent.
- Dynamic guidance schedule: source-branch CFG fixed (w=3); target-branch CFG increases monotonically (cosine schedule, 8->12) over the 50 edit steps, weak-then-strong.
- Results: beats DDIM-Inv/DDPM-Inv/SDEdit baselines on FAD/KL/SSIM with competitive CLAP, 64.5% faster than inversion baselines, positive MOS.

## What this means for us
- Confirms rather than changes SAO's roadmap: this is the DDPM-mel-spectrogram analog of FlowEdit, and the authors explicitly justify their extra machinery (re-noising + reverse-dynamics contrast rather than raw prediction contrast) as compensating for diffusion's curved SDE paths - a problem SA3's straight-line rectified-flow paths don't have. Net implication of the paper's own argument: for SA3, plain flow-direction contrast (i.e., FlowEdit, already shortlisted in the sourcebook) remains the right-shaped tool; this paper gives no reason to build the heavier DDPM-specific variant.
- Triple disconnect from SA3's construction: (1) built on ancestral/DDPM reverse dynamics (alpha_t cumulative noise, scheduler-step Phi_theta), no RF z0|t/velocity reformulation attempted; (2) both backbones are mel+vocoder, not waveform-VAE/SAME-latent, so none of the re-noising math operates on anything like our 256-dim/~10.77Hz latent; (3) needs live, per-step CFG with an increasing target scale across 50 denoising steps - inert on SA3's APT-distilled rf_denoiser checkpoint, would only be runnable on the multi-step "-base" checkpoint.
- No new control representation or empirical finding here that isn't already accounted for by SAO's existing FlowEdit/AudEdit coverage and shipped SDEdit-based longform re-anchor machinery. Worth a one-line pointer in knowledge.md ("DDPM-side sibling of FlowEdit, confirms RF's straight paths already avoid the problem it solves") but not a deep read or an implementation target - remains fully SAO's call to keep developing the RF-native FlowEdit lineage instead.
