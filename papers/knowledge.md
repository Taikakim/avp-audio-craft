# papers/ — the never-reinvent-again file

**Check here BEFORE building a training loss, guidance method, or optimizer variant.**
This is the *external* prior-art index; `ARCHITECTURE.md` is the *internal* reuse index.
Per paper: the PDF (`arxiv-<id>.pdf`) + a project-POV abstract (`arxiv-<id>.md`) stating
what it contains AND what it does *not* (i.e. what remains ours). Keep abstracts honest
about provenance: "verified citation pass" ≠ "deep-read".

**Gemini deep-research reports are VERIFY-BEFORE-BUILD — and verification cuts BOTH ways.**
Every cited paper earns its knowledge.md row from the *PDF*, never from the report's
paraphrase. But a **missing web footprint is UNVERIFIED, never "fabricated"**: double-blind
workshop submissions leave no search trace and verifier tools hit Cloudflare/OpenReview
blocks. Worked example — **DC-SAE / "Dual-Contrastive Sparse Autoencoders"** (Chen/Cherep/
Maes/Singh, MIT, ICML'26 Mech-Interp Workshop) was wrongly called "likely fabricated" on
2026-07-30 (zero footprint + blocked OpenReview id), then confirmed **real** minutes later
when Kim produced the PDF — a false-fabrication verdict, the mirror-image of the presence-
from-proxies trap. So: absence of footprint proves nothing; get the PDF. **Now 2-for-2
(2026-08-01):** the second no-footprint flag — "Dimension-Corrected Hitting Times" (Zongmin
Liu, Stanford; `arXiv:submit/7812416`) — also resolved **real** on Kim's fetch, so the
"unverified ≠ fabricated" clause is empirically earned. The three agent-invisible-but-human-
checkable patterns to expect: **(a) double-blind venue** (no arXiv id by design), **(b)
submission-stage preprint** (`arXiv:submit/…`, no public id yet), **(c) ResearchGate-only**
(RG bot-blocks verifiers). All three are zero-footprint yet human-verifiable in seconds — a
no-footprint result means ESCALATE-TO-HUMAN-FETCH, never a fabricated-verdict. Existence aside,
the attributed *claims* also drift even in genuine papers — the same audit caught real metric
misattributions (FAD↔Audiobox/LPAPS, FAD↔FMD) and a bottleneck-depth slip (report said
"2–4", truth is 2 layers {12,13}). So verify the *claim* against the PDF too, not just the
paper's existence. (The index held the correct {12,13} while the report drifted — doing its
job.) Related convention: brief Gemini with problem + phenomenology only — never feed it our
own citations or docstrings, or it closes the loop and cites them back as if external.

## Underfit memo — CFG dynamics during LoRA training (Kim 2026-07-09, important)
- **CFG optimum MOVES with training stage.** Early ('getting it'): high CFG (~7) demos light up
  first — style on a coherent prompt. Past the elbow: CFG=7 OVER-COOKS (artifacted/over-saturated),
  and LOW CFG (~1) becomes cleanest. If CFG=1 good & CFG=7 bad => LoRA has internalised the style,
  no longer needs prompt-classifier guidance.
- **Conditional→unconditional crossover.** Later, even EMPTY-prompt demos sound like the style =
  the model ABSORBED the dataset (a diagnostic for memorisation).
- **Don't fear a memorised checkpoint.** Overfitting only hurts if chasing creative variation. A
  memorised ckpt is still useful: weaken LoRA strength at inference, AND a2a/style-transfer hits
  HARDER with a memorised model (strong style signal pulls input decisively into the training dist).
- **IMPLICATION FOR OUR AVP WORK:** we rendered EVERYTHING at fixed cfg ~5. Our late-epoch
  'thin/over-sharp/hissy/breathy' renders may be CFG=7-style over-cooking at a stage that wants
  low CFG. Late ckpts should be re-rendered at cfg 1-2. And the 'overtrained' late ckpts may be
  EXCELLENT for a2a even where they're bad for txt2audio. (ARC-distilled demos lag base RF by a few
  k steps but end cleaner.)
