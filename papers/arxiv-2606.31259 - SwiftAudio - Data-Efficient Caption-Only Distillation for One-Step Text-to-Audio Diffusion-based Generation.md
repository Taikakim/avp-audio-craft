# SwiftAudio: Data-Efficient Caption-Only Distillation for One-Step Text-to-Audio Diffusion-based Generation (2606.31259v1) — light pass

Mai, Le, Dinh, Tran (PTIT Hanoi), 2026.
PDF: `2606.31259v1-SwiftAudio- Data-Efficient Caption-Only_Distillation for One-Step Text-to-Audio_Diffusion-based Generation.pdf`.
A DDPM/mel-spectrogram one-step distillation method (Auffusion teacher, VSD-based) that trains without any paired audio — captions only — plus a cheap temporal-TV latent regularizer; architecturally disjoint from SA3 (DDPM not RF, mel+vocoder not waveform-VAE) but the two ideas are worth a passing note.

## What it contains
- Distills a pretrained multi-step Auffusion (DDPM, mel-spectrogram-VAE + vocoder) teacher into a **one-step** text-to-audio student using **Variational Score Distillation (VSD)**: a frozen teacher plus an alternately-trained LoRA "fake-score" teacher supply a score-difference gradient (classic SDS/VSD form, Eq. 3), and critically **no ground-truth audio is used at all** — training data is ~45K AudioCaps *captions* only.
- Adds an **L1 temporal-TV regularizer** on the student's latent time axis: penalizes frame-to-frame latent differences (piecewise-smooth, edge-preserving, sparsity-inducing), which the ablation shows measurably helps FAD/IS over no-regularization and over an L2 smoothness variant.
- Claims SOTA among strict one-step TTA baselines (AudioLCM, ConsistencyTTA) on AudioCaps/Clotho, and demonstrates the distilled one-step student retains disentangled semantic control (word-swap, attention-reweighting, word-refinement edits on frozen noise z) inherited from the teacher.
- Explicitly a DDPM pipeline throughout: fixed noise schedule {α_t, σ_t}, CFG guidance scale 7.5 for both teachers, ε-prediction noise predictor reparameterized into a one-step map (Eq. 1), and generation returns `V(D(x̂0))` — VAE decoder then vocoder over a mel-spectrogram latent (C×F×W = 4×32×128).

## What this means for us
- Fails both of our hard filters at once: it's DDPM score-matching (not rectified flow — the VSD score-difference gradient would need re-derivation as a velocity/z0|t-difference under SA3's straight-line flow) *and* it operates on a mel-spectrogram VAE + vocoder latent (not the 256-dim/10.77Hz waveform-VAE SAME latent). No drop-in path.
- Doesn't change anything about the diffusion-control/guidance/attention stack (CFG, APG, LatCH, FusionCC, HeadRouter, etc.) — orthogonal topic (training-time distillation, not sample-time control), and SA3's rf_denoiser checkpoints are CFG-inert anyway, so a CFG-scale-7.5 VSD teacher setup as-is wouldn't even apply to our few-step checkpoints (would need our multi-step/"-base" CFG-live checkpoint as the teacher, which is at least consistent with the paper's teacher role).
- The one genuinely portable idea, low-cost to note: **audio-free / caption-only distillation** as a concept — if SAO ever wanted to build a *new* one-step distillation pipeline without spending on paired audio-latent training data, VSD-with-LoRA-fake-teacher is a documented mechanism, though it would need full re-derivation for RF and for SAME's continuous latent (no discrete mel-VAE bottleneck to exploit the way Auffusion's does). Given SAO already ships APT-distilled few-step checkpoints, this isn't solving a live problem.
- The **L1 temporal-TV regularizer** on generated latent frame-differences is a two-line auxiliary loss that's architecture-agnostic and could be tried against z0_hat trajectories in any of SAO's own consistency/distillation work for cheap coherence gains — worth a footnote in the book's distillation/consistency notes, nothing more.
- Net: low relevance, correctly filtered out by both hard rules; recorded here only so we don't re-discover the same "audio-free VSD distillation" idea from scratch later without knowing it was already tried (on a different architecture) and what its main levers were (LoRA-rank of the fake teacher strongly gates quality; TV beats L2 smoothness for latent regularization).
