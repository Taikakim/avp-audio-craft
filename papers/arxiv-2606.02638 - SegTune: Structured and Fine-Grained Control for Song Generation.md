# SegTune: Structured and Fine-Grained Control for Song Generation (2606.02638) — light pass

Wang, Ji, Cai, Li, Zheng, Song, Liu, Zhang, Wan (Kling Team, Kuaishou/USTC/PKU), 31 May 2026.
PDF: `2606.02638v1-SegTune Structured and Fine-Grained Control for Song Generation.pdf`.
A song-generation DiT (vocals+lyrics+accompaniment) built on genuine flow-matching and the Stable-Audio-lineage 1D VAE; its main transferable idea is a hierarchical global+segment natural-language text conditioning pathway, broadcast per time-window and channel-concatenated — a third pattern alongside SAO's trained-probe (LatCH) and frozen-meter-consistency (FusionCC) approaches to structure/emotion control.

## What it contains
- DiT (1.1B, LLaMA-style blocks) trained with conditional flow matching (x_t=(1-t)x0+t*x1, target velocity u=x1-x0) — linear-interpolation rectified flow, not DDPM — on a 1D VAE latent at 21.5Hz (Evans et al. 2024a, the direct predecessor of Stable Audio's own latent-diffusion lineage).
- Hierarchical text conditioning: a global prompt (genre/timbre/emotion) broadcast across the whole song, and per-segment prompts (instrumentation/rhythm/emotional-transition, e.g. "verse", "chorus") broadcast only within their own time window as determined by a duration predictor. Both are encoded by the same Qwen3-Embedding-0.6B LLM text encoder, then channel-concatenated (not summed/mixed) and projected via an MLP into the DiT's conditioning embedding.
- A separately fine-tuned Qwen3-4B-Base "duration predictor" LLM that autoregressively emits sentence-level LRC-format timestamps at inference, removing the need for manual lyric-duration annotation (prior NAR song systems needed ground-truth or fragile zero-shot timestamps).
- Ablations: channel-concatenation of global+segment embeddings beats both a global-only baseline and a linearly-mixed-weight fusion; a general LLM text encoder (Qwen3-Embedding) beats a music-specialized contrastive encoder (Muq-MuLan) specifically for singer gender/age control, because MuLan's training captions never encoded vocal identity (t-SNE shows near-total gender overlap for MuLan vs. clean separation for Qwen3-Embedding).
- New eval metrics: segment-level MuLan score, singer gender/age control accuracy via an audio-LLM judge.

## What this means for us
- Different task substantially: full song generation with lyrics/vocals is outside SA3's scope (no lyrics-to-vocal conditioning pathway in SA3 per current architecture), so the duration-predictor / LRC-alignment half of the paper doesn't transfer.
- What is transferable: a concrete, working instance of "natural-language segment-level conditioning broadcast per time-window" as a local-additive-style control signal — distinct from LatCH's trained numeric probes and from FusionCC's t-gated consistency-against-a-frozen-meter. Worth a note in the book's local-additive/structure-control material as a third design pattern for future SAO work: instead of training a probe head, encode a per-segment text caption with an LLM embedder and broadcast it into the corresponding latent time window, concatenated (not summed) with the global-style embedding.
- Minor but reusable empirical finding: channel-concatenation of multi-scale (global+local) conditioning embeddings clearly outperforms linear/weighted mixing in their ablations — relevant to any SAO experiment that fuses global style conditioning with local/structural conditioning.
- Not a correction to anything SAO has shipped or found; this is a corroborating pattern from an adjacent task (full song generation), not a new SOTA mechanism for SA3's actual instrumental/text-to-audio setting. Deep-read not warranted — the paper's core novelty (lyric duration prediction, LRC alignment, singer-attribute data pipeline) is vocal/lyrics-specific and doesn't bear on SA3.
