**Relevance to SAO: conceptual cross-reference only — symbolic-MIDI, whole-piece (non-time-resolved) tonal-profile fingerprinting via persistent homology; not a control mechanism and not directly portable to an audio-domain, time-resolved, trainable pipeline like ours.**
**Status: DEEP-READ (full PDF, 2026-08-12, GHOST-NOTE).**

The method: take the *Tonnetz* — the classical neo-Riemannian graph of pitch-class relationships, here formalized as an infinite planar simplicial complex (0-simplices = pitch classes, 1-simplices = perfect-fifth/major-third/minor-third edges, 2-simplices = major/minor triads) — and fold its periodicity into a finite torus complex 𝕋. Given a musical phrase (a finite set of notes, each an (pitch, duration) pair in seconds), every vertex `v` of 𝕋 labeled with pitch-class `[p]` is displaced along a third axis by `d_v = Σ d_i`, the **sum of durations of every note in the phrase whose pitch belongs to `[p]`**, then this displacement is extended linearly over the simplices to make a deformed polyhedral surface `𝒯` embedded in ℝ³. A height function `f` (the z-coordinate, i.e. total-duration-per-pitch-class) induces a lower-level-set filtration of `𝒯`, and the paper computes 0th and 1st **persistent homology** (persistence diagrams `D₀`, `D₁`) of that filtration, comparing diagrams between pieces via the **bottleneck distance** `d_B(D,D') = min_σ max_p d(p,σ(p))` over point-bijections.

Musical readout of the diagrams: `D₀` (connected components) — the single point at infinity's abscissa `ū` is the height-function minimum; `ū≈0` means at least one pitch class is essentially unused (tonal/modal piece), `ū>>0` means all 12 pitch classes carry substantial duration (atonal/chromatic). Proper points in `D₀` mark chromatic pitch-class clusters not linked by a Tonnetz edge (max 3 connected components possible, since the torus needs adjacent-semitone clusters to fragment further). `D₁` (1-dimensional "tunnels") always carries exactly two points at infinity (the torus's two fundamental loop generators); their mutual distance shrinks under heavy chromatic use, and extra proper points flag maxima of `f` forming disconnected subcomplexes — read as atonality. Worked examples: Debussy's *Arabesque* (`D₀` no proper points → pentatonic/diatonic), Ravel's *Jeux d'Eau* (high `ū`, one `D₀` proper point identified by the authors as the "Petrushka chord," a major triad + its tritone-substitute superposed), Schönberg's *Klavierstück 1* (two `D₀` proper points → atonal). The paper also shows a concrete limitation motivating the whole weighted-height construction: the *plain* (undeformed) Tonnetz subcomplex approach from prior work (ref [2]) maps the Lydian and Locrian modes to **isomorphic** subcomplexes — i.e., un-weighted graph topology alone cannot tell those two modes apart; adding the duration-weighted height function is the fix.

Four empirical demonstrations, all via bottleneck-distance hierarchical clustering / dendrograms, **none with a quantitative accuracy/F1 number against ground truth** — every verdict is qualitative dendrogram-reading:
1. A 9-piece tonal/atonal set (Beethoven, Debussy, Mozart, Ravel, Schönberg): `D₀`-distance clustering cleanly separates Schönberg's two *Klavierstücke* + Ravel's *Jeux d'Eau* from a Mozart/Beethoven/Debussy cluster; Beethoven's pentatonic 3rd movement clusters with Debussy's *Arabesque*.
2. Three performances of "All the Things You Are" (quartet / piano solo / piano+bass), segmented into intro/theme/solo/coda sections, plus transposed and randomized (shuffled-pitch) variants. Transposed versions land at **bottleneck distance exactly zero** from their originals (the filtration is invariant to uniform pitch-class shift on the torus). Randomized versions form their own clean separate cluster from all real musical fragments — the method distinguishes structured music from scrambled pitches. Real segments cluster loosely by intervallic/rhythmic similarity.
3. 58 pop songs by 28 artists (Ray Charles to Lady Gaga), `D₁`-distance clustering into 3 groups — described by the authors themselves only as "promising," with no external genre/style ground truth, no accuracy metric, and the write-up is essentially "here is which artist's songs land in which cluster."

**What it does NOT contain:**
- **No audio-domain implementation.** Every experiment is on MIDI/symbolic note lists. Section 5 says extending to audio via a chromagram-derived height function "is straightforward" and that bottleneck-distance stability should absorb chroma-extraction noise — but this is an unverified claim, not a result; nothing in the paper touches a waveform, a spectrogram, or a learned chroma extractor.
- **No time-resolved / sequential signal at all**, despite the paper's own claim in §3.2 to capture "both temporal and harmonic information": `d_v` is a **sum of durations per pitch class over the whole phrase** — a static duration-weighted pitch-class histogram embedded on a torus, not a trajectory. Two pieces with identical total per-pitch-class duration but completely different note orderings would deform the Tonnetz identically. This is a load-bearing precision point: the method is a *global tonal fingerprint per clip/phrase*, not a per-frame or per-beat signal — the opposite of what a LatCH-style control head (which needs a target *sequence*, e.g. `onset_density(t)`) requires.
- **No quantitative classification metrics anywhere** — no accuracy/precision/recall/F1, no comparison against a chroma-vector-Euclidean-distance baseline, no statistical test on the clustering quality. All four "results" are dendrogram-shape narratives.
- **No learned/trainable component.** Purely a hand-built geometric construction + off-the-shelf persistent-homology + bottleneck-distance pipeline; nothing here is differentiable or drop-in as a training-time loss.
- **No rhythm/meter/beat structure** beyond aggregate per-pitch-class duration — no onset timing, no tempo, no downbeat information (the features SAO's own onset/beat LatCH heads target are simply absent from this model).
- The authors' own explicitly stated future work (§5) — augmenting Tonnetz dimensionality, multidimensional persistent homology, **time-varying** analysis — confirms the time-resolved gap above is a known limitation, not an oversight on our part.

Net: interesting as a *conceptual* precedent for "topologically fingerprint a tonal profile and compare fingerprints via a formal metric," and mildly relevant to the team's chroma/NNLS/HPCP tooling as a possible offline whole-clip tonal-consistency check (e.g., comparing a generated clip's duration-weighted pitch-class profile against a reference via bottleneck distance, as an alternative to raw chroma cosine). But it is symbolic-only, non-time-resolved, non-differentiable, decade-old (2016), evaluated only qualitatively on small hand-picked datasets (9 pieces / 3 song versions / 58 pop songs), and does not touch control, feature extraction for generation, or any evaluation metric that would slot into the current LatCH/control-adapter/onset-eval machinery without a substantial re-design. Not actionable for control-method design or per-frame evaluation metrics as currently scoped.
