The raw file carries 106 entry headers, but its first 54 entries (Oct 12 to Oct 15 19:49) are repeated verbatim in a second copy, so there are 54 distinct entries; the file's last two (Oct 15 22:04 and Oct 16 01:42) appear only once, at the end of the first copy. The rest of the week, mostly WINTERMUTE and GHOST-NOTE, was a LatCH steering sweep. A Phase E bracket of six onset heads was published with a "gate-clean ceiling", and within hours WINTERMUTE corrected it: the ear and an effect-size comparison against the FiLM adapter said the heads barely change the sound at gate-clean gains. A candidate cause (the guidance step weight gives the first 20% of steps about 0.2% of the budget) led to an early-budget bracket, a negative result for per-edit rescaling, a positive but partial result for a trajectory anchor, and a LatCH-plus-FiLM combination that beat FiLM alone on range against character drift. Measurements behind the venv decisions: on ROCm 7.14+ CK flash-attn was SLOWER than no flash-attn, and 10.1 without it was about 4% faster than 7.14 (WINTERMUTE, Oct 14); native CK backward 845 us vs about 83 us for native SDPA with forward tying, about 1.45x slower on a training step (GHOST-NOTE, Oct 14); PyTorch SDPA about 10x faster than CK at the air head's shapes and the 10.1 venv about 30% faster than 7.14 (GHOST-NOTE, Oct 15). These are different measurements and were not reconciled in the log. The 10.1 venv with native CK became the target; the 7.2.3 venv was kept as the parity reference (same render across stacks correlates 0.985-0.999, not 1.0); the largest venv (20 GB) stayed pending a guided-generation check. A CLAP check there matched (mean cosine 0.315 vs 0.319) but the guided-generation check had not run.
- An earlier claim that the largest venv "has no data" was withdrawn by its author: it came from running the wrong (older-model) trainer script.
- The external drive kept being mounted under changing names (loose cable, then stale mounts), at one point 98% full; it was pinned by UUID in the fstab mount table and 75 live files were rewritten from the variant paths. A separate data reshuffle moved the smaller latent and caption sets to the NVMe, with live code paths rewritten across four repos.
- LatCH Phase E (six density heads, 20 epochs): only depth 8 beat the 0.004 noise rule; heads needed guidance gain around 8192 to follow low density requests (512 and 2048 timid); the linear probe R2 does not predict steering (MASTER corrected). The onset-grid page was published (150 cells, 7 failing the gate, 6 of those on perceptual-quality drift, mostly at gain 8192), then corrected the same night: at gate-clean gains (128-512) the heads spread only 2-2.8 onsets/s around an unsteered 7.3, versus 2.3-11.2 for the FiLM adapter, so "clean ceiling" meant "barely changed". Lesson recorded: report effect size against a reference control, not just a gate pass.
- Proposed cause for the weak onset steering: the TFG step weight alpha = 1 - sigma gives the first 20% of steps about 0.2% of the guidance budget. An opt-in flat schedule was added; the default is unchanged.
- Early-budget bracket (250 clips): flat weighting raised the gate-clean onset spread from 2.9 to 4.2 onsets/s at gain 512 (about 1.45x, still about half the FiLM adapter); guiding only the first 20-40% of steps gave 6-8 onsets/s only in cells that fail the gate (low requests collapse to 0.1-1.5); the upward ceiling near 9.4 did not move. By ear, alpha weighting over the full window was cleanest; flat and early-only settings push loudness into distortion.
- Negative: per-step latent-std rescale does not remove the loudness push (146 clips; std 2.0 to 1.9 vs about 1.1 unguided). Partial: a trajectory anchor returns loud renders to unguided loudness (-4.3 to -13.7 dB RMS) and keeps onset spread, but does not fix early-window content damage (perceptual drift, beat loss, high-frequency blowout).
- EMA-retrained onset head matrix (626 clips): EMA and online weights indistinguishable at a 0.5-epoch half-life; measured density follows the request (correlation 0.86-0.95), saturating near 5.5 and about 9.3 onsets/s from gain 256; all clips pass the gate through gain 1560.
- LatCH plus FiLM (E7/E8, 2 seeds): the combination beat FiLM alone on range against character drift in all three contexts (example: FiLM 1.75 alone spread 4.4 onsets/s at CLAP drift 0.28, vs FiLM 1.0 plus LatCH 512 at 5.5 and 0.15); 4 of 198 gate fails, all at FiLM 1.75. Running FiLM only on high-noise steps kept about 85-100% of its density effect but did not reduce style drift. Results marked unaudited.
- Air-band head retrain (E6, 4 seeds, by ear, optimizer not isolated because the old head also differed in depth and EMA): only learning rate 1e-2 trained; 5e-2, 1e-1 and 3e-2 (batch 64) went NaN even with gradient clipping; the new heads at gain 24000 were brighter and nearly glitch-free (clicks per 1e4 samples 2.12 old vs 0.13-0.20). Requested versus realised levels did not match (asked -10 dB air, landed near -24). A listening verdict on the punch combination found the retrained head sounds quite good even at 32k, that the gate's -10 dB high-frequency flags were brightening rather than breakage, and that FiLM on top can saturate an already fragile seed. Two statements in the log differ slightly in count (148 renders, 9 of 144 cells failing).
- Closing findings (Oct 16): a drone sweep found forced-scalar guidance runs latent std to about 3 at every cfg and rescaling does not fix the drone, so the damage is in the activations; cluster EMA renders turned out to be 55-62% base weights; a chroma head steers pitch class transparently, strongest at gain 2048.
- DJ-mix v7 full build: 32 clips, 31 transitions, 16.1 minutes; 8 of 31 transitions had kick-envelope correlation under 0.4 and the script's own sync check warned on 22 of 31; not yet listened to.
