# Week 2026-W28 synopsis (Jul 06 – Jul 12)
*309 entries · the week audio-to-audio transitions became usable — and we learned why they hadn't been*

The fleet spent the week chasing one musical goal — making audio-to-audio (a2a) transitions and style-transfer sound good rather than mushy — and, in chasing it, built a mechanistic account of why the mid-noise regime fails. The headline landing was harmony-steered "chroma-morph" transitions: guiding a generated bridge to follow the source's harmony produced a crossfade the project lead called one of the coolest things he'd heard in nearly 30 years of music, and a bar-snapped operating point (window ~1025, noise 0.42, chroma on) was judged "completely usable." Underneath it, three independent methods converged on the same story: rhythm is a noise-invariant feature the model rebuilds in mid-stack layers, while harmony and melody are destroyed and abandoned under mid-range noise and filled with corpus-generic filler — so harmony must be re-supplied externally, exactly what chroma guidance does. In parallel, a long diagnosis of the own-music adapters (reported as "glitchy") worked through and discarded several hypotheses before landing on conditioning collapse from single-caption training as the primary disease, with caption diversity — not the trigger word — as the cure. The week wasn't all wins: a scalar "hardness" steering head was shown to buzz because it had learned a distortion shortcut, an apparent "least-static" breakthrough was falsified the moment it hit human ears (the metric measured motion, not musicality), and two training-free long-form-coherence fixes from the literature both came back empirically null on our stack. The team closed the week with a cross-model listening board and a 31,646-clip metrics database that turned scattered impressions into distributional verdicts.

## Headlines
- **Chroma-morph transitions validated by ear** — harmony-steered a2a bridges cleared the "usable" bar; seam-inpainting dropped from the recipe, 512-frame windows the sweet spot, longer windows read worse.
- **Mid-band a2a mechanism solved** — melodic movement bottoms out at noise 0.40–0.55 and overshoots at 0.70; a layer×feature map shows rhythm is rebuilt (noise-invariant) at DiT blocks ~11–15 while harmony/melody is abandoned — the mechanistic case for chroma guidance.
- **Own-music adapter diagnosis** — degradation separates into three processes: monotonic spectral collapse (zero-crossing rate as a clean overtraining odometer), a U-shaped tempo instability that's optimization-phase-driven (rank-128 removes it; rank-16 mode-hops), and immediate conditioning collapse; tiered captions restore prompt sensitivity (ratio 1.5–2.65 vs 0.22 for single-caption).
- **Training rule of thumb, corroborated three ways** — rank-128 trains best and tolerates adapter weight >1.0; rank-16 is harsher (more HF/distortion), lower quality (Audiobox CE/PQ), and fragile at high weight; high-rank quality peaks early (~ep0–4) then overtrains.
- **Mechanistic interpretability + training-free steering** — acoustic attributes causally localize to late self-attention/feed-forward blocks (16–23), not the cross-attention bottleneck seen in prior work; mood "concept directions" peak at layers 8–15 (third confirmation of a mid-stack semantic bottleneck) and drive audible, training-free steering (a "dark" score 0.018→0.341, ~19×).
- **Breathing-noise controller built, TDD'd, and validated** — a self-calibrated hysteresis controller that lowers noise where a2a output loops; +13% novelty on breathed windows. Follow-on finding: noise scheduling alone can't buy "creative and crisp" — conditioning richness is the real lever.
- **Negative results logged in ink** — scalar hardness head confirmed as a distortion shortcut (Gram-Schmidt fix path noted); the prompt-arc "breakthrough" failed the ear test (metric ≠ musicality); Incantation-mask and RoPE-jitter long-form fixes both null (loop is self-attention continuing the clamped prefix).
- **Cross-model listening board + 31.6k-clip metrics DB** — a big model×checkpoint×cfg×strength matrix rendered overnight, plus a queryable 14-metric database; both surfaced that ~25 models were barely tested and pushed verdicts toward distributional "good-fraction per checkpoint" rather than binary good/bad.
- **Alpha-scaling audit** — the odd rank/alpha pairs were a deliberate rsLoRA-style schedule applied linearly, i.e. monotone adapter damping — which explains both "better at higher epochs" on high-rank runs and the near-universal preference for inference weight 1.5.
- **Infrastructure hardened** — self-healing presence listeners, public mirroring of instance chat/DMs with path masking, a "have we built this already?" discoveries index, per-run task ledgers, and HPC campaign scaffolding (job arrays, recipe fixes) staged for the first real supercomputer jobs.
