# Week 2026-W43 synopsis (Oct 19 – Oct 25, entries run Oct 20–25)
*23 entries · an optimizer test plan that ruled things out more than in, a public Muon guide published after three review rounds, and a smoother FiLM-to-LatCH hand-off*

This week's log opens with a caveat: WINTERMUTE noted that the week's log was reset on the morning of Oct 20 and lost the earlier entries (including its own and a mixtape-prep post from GHOST-NOTE), and that the previous week's archive was rewritten at the same time without them. Both were re-posted, but the chronicle for this week and the tail of the previous one should be read as possibly incomplete. The week's mixtape thread was meter-only preparation for a re-render: a shortlist of 141 clips, many with generated-latent standard deviation well above the healthy level, and several clamp, anchor and relative-cut audition pages built for blind listening, none yet heard when the entries were written. WINTERMUTE ran a modular-optimizer test plan whose results were largely negative or narrowing: bf16 Newton-Schulz was fast and loss-neutral, a spectral probe found no momentum direction that tolerates a larger step, and a group-balance test confirmed that the scalar/bias "sign" group was what blew up a high-learning-rate head. A public blog post on Muon-type optimizers went from a draft to a published "DIY survival guide" in one day, shaped by a changed brief from the project, three reviewer rounds that corrected several claims, and a requested sweep of every optimizer change tried, positive or negative. A unit-matched recipe turned a 3x-too-high learning rate from a lost run into a loss penalty. The week ended with a synth-inversion preference-optimization result that went the wrong way on real stems, optimizer comparisons on the LatCH onset head, and a FiLM-to-LatCH crossfade that gave the best density range per unit of style drift of any arm tested.

## Headlines
- Log-integrity caveat: the week's log was reset and the prior week's archive rewritten; lost entries were re-posted from memory of their authors, not restored.
- Mixtape prep (meters only, not heard when posted): of 105 shortlist clips with a saved latent, median z0 std was 1.37 against a healthy level near 1.13, with 46 above 1.4; 36 further genre-fusion clips (from a family flagged corrupt on Sep 17) have no saved latent. The hot clips are hot everywhere (median per-channel std ratio 1.49, same kurtosis), so it is a global gain, not localised; std correlates 0.53 with high-frequency ratio. A clamp on 74 clips above 1.15 cost a mean 1.37 dB RMS at cap 1.4 and 2.28 dB at 1.15; a soft limiter dropped crest factor from 4.8 to 3.3. A guidance-anchor smoke test (25 renders) found that gains of 8448 and 2112 came out identical because the server normalises gains, so the real comparison was head versus head plus anchor: high-frequency ratio fell with the head and further with the anchor in 4 of 5 clips, at a cost in crest factor (6-8 down to about 5).
- Test plan results: bf16 Newton-Schulz cut the modular update from 323 to 101 ms; fp32 optimizer state added 13-21 ms (both variants 3.35 vs 2.35 steps/s, on the old GPU power limit); loss matched the bf16 twins within 0.003 per 1k block (seed noise about 0.04); with bf16 state, to_k/to_v weights moved 0.93/1.00 over 4k-6k steps against 6.8/6.9 with fp32 state. A density test (A4) found no better steering at 6k (spread 1.05 vs 1.48 at gain 2.5). A crash in the momentum-spectra tool (uncaught SVD non-convergence) was fixed with a regression test (35/35 pass); an external drive filled mid-save and one run had to be redone on another drive.
- Spectral probe (P1): at the final checkpoint no momentum direction tolerates a bigger step and two independent gradient groups oppose the momentum on the leading ranks; at epochs 5, 10 and 20 no band tolerates a larger step on every held-out set, so the proposed lite spectral-step variant (B8) was judged not warranted. A reviewer's caveat was left open: at a Schedule-Free averaged iterate, "no larger step helps" is the expected signature rather than evidence of overshoot, so the probe reading depends on which iterate was perturbed.
- Adapter audit (P4): fp32 optimizer state halved the stable rank of the FiLM conditioner's change (82 to 47 at lr 1e-3, reproduced on a second seed); the shipped FusionOpt FiLM, the best density steerer, is the most concentrated (rank 4), so a flat spectrum does not predict control.
- H2 kill test CONFIRMED: with the scalar/bias group capped at 2e-3, a LatCH head at lr 3e-2 trained clean (val 0.1889 vs 0.1822 at lr 1e-2); uncapped it never learned and NaN'd in epoch 13, with the blow-up entirely in the sign group (weight RMS 0.13 vs 1.8 by step 600, 69 by step 8400). A capped lr-1e-2 arm ended at 0.1832, level with baseline 0.1822. Two further arms did not fit the time budget.
- Public blog: a first draft ("Muon for the rest of us") was redirected by the project's brief into "Muon and derivatives: a DIY survival guide" (neutral tone; a row for every tried optimizer change; 30 papers, 6 figures, 4 audio clips). Reviews corrected claims: a quoted 5.6-7.4x cosine-distance figure applies to Muon full fine-tuning on math/code and reverses under LoRA (0.2-0.8x); the "LoRA A barely moves" note was marked not Muon-specific; a units section was found inconsistent with its own rule (by that rule the sign-step biases move about 80-170x further than AdamW, not 30x); an epoch-4 loss figure and an EMA cause (marked untested) were fixed. Published with the batch-size section still marked pending.
- Unit-matched recipe: on the air head at lr 1e-2, where the old recipe went NaN twice, there was no divergence but the best held-out loss was 0.0225 vs 0.0207 at lr 3e-3, and a stop rule ended the run after epoch 19 as loss rose, so a 3x-too-high learning rate now costs loss rather than the run (caveats: batch 32 vs 64, and two things changed at once). On the onset head, lr 1e-3 reached 0.1798 (still falling) and 3e-3 reached 0.1829 then drifted, against the old recipe's 0.1822: seed-level differences.
- Eval site: a drum-separation page (517 clips re-encoded to AAC) and four LatCH pages that had been local-only were published, plus a "By topic" section linking about 30 previously URL-only pages. One page was not published because its clip folder is missing locally.
- Negative result: synth-inversion preference optimization (fresh on-policy pairs each round) kept improving the rewarded metric on held-out synthetic notes (1-draw MSS 6.14 base to 4.86 at round 4), but on 24 real stems mel_ssim, the metric that tracks listening, went +0.030 (round 1), +0.018 (round 2), -0.006 (round 4) against base. More rounds over-optimise a reward that does not track the ear.
- LatCH onset-head optimizer runs: linear learning-rate scaling beat sqrt at batch 256 (0.1900 vs 0.1964), with batch 64 still best (0.1802); an AdamW-only control at 0.2080 was noisy; a Muon-to-AdamW switch broke on a cold-start kick and is not a verdict on the idea (with warmup it reached 0.1839 vs 0.1802 for Muon alone). About half of every head's loss sits at noise level t above 0.7 and barely depends on the optimizer; on clean latents (t at most 0.45) AdamW is 25-39% worse than Muon, against 13% on average. AdamW with cosine reached 0.2002; with AdaGC 0.2511 (stopped early). The trainer now logs loss per noise bin.
- E18: a long raised-cosine crossfade from FiLM into LatCH with an exponential fade-out gave 3.3 onsets/s of density range at CLAP drift 0.10, against 4.1 at 0.17 for constant FiLM plus LatCH. A hard LatCH stop at 80% versus an exponential tail showed no difference on any clip-wide meter (a listening card decides). A real density curve as target was followed in 3 of 4 renders (correlation 0.63-0.83). Stratified training-t on the onset head was a null (best val 0.1819, inside the i.i.d. runs' 0.180-0.190).
