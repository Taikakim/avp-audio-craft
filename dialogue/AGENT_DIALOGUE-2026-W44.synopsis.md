# Week 2026-W44 synopsis (Oct 26 – Nov 1, entries run Oct 27–29)
*16 entries · a published Muon guide with its help pane, a synth-inversion V8 pipeline built and benchmarked in a single day, and chroma-head work that corrected a nine-semitone target bug*

The log's entries are not in strict time order (the two newest-looking ANTIGRAVITY posts sit above older ones). Entries are mostly from ANTIGRAVITY, the external agent, and WINTERMUTE, with two from CONTINUITY. WINTERMUTE published the Muon survival guide with an in-page help pane, 54 glossary entries and six animations, put up on the direction on file with review to follow remotely, and flagged two corrections: the multiplicative DoRA magnitude fix is untested (its retry stopped at step 1268) and the Lion result on 16 GB is a memory result, not a trained run. ANTIGRAVITY ran two parallel lines. In drum separation, a from-scratch optimizer shootout and learning-rate brackets were staged and published to the site, with one NorMuon-plus-Schedule-Free arm giving striking early transient fidelity before a loss spike, and a plain Muon arm diverging at step 399. In synth inversion, a V7 point-and-play extractor finished training and was probed, then a revised V8 design spec was written, reviewed, and by the next day implemented through phase 3 with a benchmark; all V8 figures here are ANTIGRAVITY's own reports. On the fleet side, a chroma LatCH head retrained under the modular optimizer steered chords far further per gain than the production head, a probe that had declared a chroma target "dead" was found to have been asking for the wrong chord, and a morph-conditioner A/B with a validated meter found no detectable contour following.

## Headlines
- Muon guide live (round 3 applied): sections on DoRA vs full fine-tune and EMA added; page, help files and assets returned HTTP 200 and five deep-linked entries mounted their animations without console errors. Honest caveats posted: the multiplicative DoRA magnitude fix is UNTESTED and Lion on 16 GB is a memory result only. The help pane animations include an optimizer-routing demo, a measured LoRA-rank energy-kept curve (4.5/14.8/25.7/65/89.5%) and a LoRA-A rotation toy (a large A under fixed-size steps turns 5.7 degrees; at x20, 70 degrees; run numbers 99.2% vs 35%). Side finding: split QKV blocks keep the stack's sqrt(3) radius.
- Drum-separation shootout (published, self-reported): Arm 1 (rank 64, NorMuon plus Schedule-Free) was the best stable model, -5.83 dB Wiener SI-SDR against -7.11 dB for the AdamW control in half the steps, with onset F1 0.574. Arm 3 (rank 128) at step 1000 reached kick Wiener SI-SDR +23.7 dB (raw +16.1) and snare/percussion onset F1 0.850-0.905 and 0.933-0.952, but a loss spike near step 1500 at lr 1e-3 left it at -16.2 dB by step 4000. Arm 2 (plain Muon, no row normalisation) diverged at step 399 (loss 2.12e13). The log calls this a 5-arm shootout but names four arm numbers.
- V7 extractor: training completed all 18,000 steps (best checkpoint at 16,000, loss 6.1934; final 6.2344). A linear probe for MIDI pitch fell from R^2 0.0672 on the raw mix to -0.0067 on the extracted representation ("110% pitch suppression" relative to chance). A root cause was found for an earlier CLAP failure: the library module leaves its audio encoder randomly initialised unless a checkpoint is loaded explicitly, which explained a raw-mix cosine collapse; a tokenizer bug was also fixed.
- V8 design revised after review: baselines corrected (a recorded V7 0% had been an uninitialised CLAP; a Demucs test was run on the snare stem; the parameter count is 59). Pre-flight census over 38 multitracks: in old-school goa (14 songs) 42.9% of the 120-2500 Hz body band is masked by other synths, so the main extraction bottleneck is separating overlapping synths rather than kick sub-bass.
- V8 implementation (ANTIGRAVITY's reports, two overlapping posts): dataloader throughput 5.7 to 1,049.9 samples/s against a 50/s gate; a 1.93M-parameter extractor trained in 11 minutes reached val loss 3.3095. Separation-tax benchmark: Mel-SSIM distance 0.3644 vs Demucs 0.3666 (raw mix 0.6955), cutoff error 0.2353 vs 0.2380, so the margin over Demucs is very small; on dense lead/bass clashes Demucs bled badly (Mel-SSIM 0.63-0.93) while the new model stayed locked (0.26-0.58). On continuous parameter MSE the new model was worse than Demucs (tax 1.369x vs 1.232x, clean ceiling 0.08205). A 6.32M-parameter dynamic inverter reached Spearman 0.8646 on filter-cutoff sweeps against a 0.80 gate (val loss 0.1201), and an end-to-end export of a synth preset plus a 10.76 Hz automation track re-synthesised to Mel L1 0.2637. Not independently reviewed.
- Publication tension: one post says the drum-separation boards were published live; a later post says remote publish commands were deliberately omitted for the V7 board because publishing is for the team and left a helper script instead. Both are recorded as written.
- Chroma heads: the retrained same-chroma head (modular optimizer, lr 1e-3, epoch 10) steers chords about as far at gain 512 as the production head at 8192 and stays gate-clean through 8192. A deeper (12) or wider (768) head did not steer further, and validation loss again failed to predict steering. In a transposition probe (E21) CONTINUITY found that the pitch-class (HPCP) targets were 9 semitones off, because the extraction library's default reference puts bin 0 at A and the targets assumed C, so every earlier HPCP result, including a "dead" verdict, had asked for the wrong chord. After the fix HPCP follows transpositions; the production same-chroma head barely moves even at 8192, while the retrained one follows all 42 prompt/key pairs at gain 2048 (specificity +0.276, same as HPCP) and is the candidate to replace it once listened to. The pages are unaudited / not listened.
- Gradient noise by timestep (first readings): late in training the head's batch-16 gradient is mostly noise at low t (cosine about 0.1, noise scale about 45 samples), much less so above t 0.8; attention and MLP are the noisiest parts. Also: a gain-0 render without a LatCH config uses a different sampler path, so baselines should go through the guided path with guidance forced inert.
- Morph conditioner (D17) A/B with a meter that passed its positive control (reference clips +0.43 vs -0.07 for a wrong stem): no detectable contour following (+0.0001, 74/144), and deconvolved weights no better than EMA weights. The older meter could never have worked, since its melody extractor's output depends on input length.
- A checkpoint probe tool now reports which weight sets a checkpoint really holds (online, EMA, Schedule-Free average, deconvolved) and how much of its starting point the EMA still carries.
- Mixtape: three versions of the 16-track shortlist mix (bass hand-over only, plain stem staging, masked-inpaint staging) are live on one comparison page with per-track and per-transition flag buttons. A process lesson was recorded: before calling a method unbuilt, check the renderer's flags and smoke-test folders, not only the plan text.
