# Week 2026-W33 synopsis (Aug 10 – Aug 16)
*173 entries · the week self-checking became the actual discipline, and the two-week deliverable list was set*

By volume this was the fleet's biggest week yet, and its real subject was not any single result but a recurring shape: a check that reports success without actually verifying the thing it claims to verify. It showed up everywhere — a piped exit code that silently discarded a lock guard, a statistical null that couldn't distinguish real signal from a comparison contaminated by its own reference, a six-crop sample too small to reach the region where a bug lived, a manifest upload filter that matched `.json` but not `.jsonl`, a median that read as "fine" while the underlying spread was not. Three different instances caught themselves making the same mistake, sometimes twice in one evening, and instead of just fixing each instance they kept generalizing the lesson until it became doctrine: "a check has to show what it would look like if the claim were false," and "print statements are not evidence." Two hardened outcomes came out of that discipline — a shared filelock tool that makes the old failure mode structurally impossible, and a mandatory latent-sanity gate that stops corrupted audio from ever reaching a published board. Underneath all of it, a large fleet-wide reading sweep of topology and music-theory papers ran for days, got its relevance criterion corrected mid-sweep by Kim (symbolic papers name control targets, they don't need to process audio to matter), and fed directly into the week's turning point: Kim set the only three goals for the next two weeks — a great-sounding AVP model, the density adapter, and a working pitch/melody control head — and everything else got explicitly parked. The back half of the week was one long, rigorously self-correcting chase to build the melody head's actual training target: the right pitch-tracking algorithm, two separate melodic voices, a genuinely broken crop-encoder found and fixed, and a real question about label noise settled with a 32-draw ensemble rather than a guess.

## Headlines
- Kim's two-week directive landed: only three deliverables (great AVP model, density adapter, functional pitch/melody head) plus the internal big goa-set model — everything else in flight gets parked.
- The melody head's actual training target got built and audited end-to-end: YIN pitch-tracking was wrong for a polyphonic lead (28% small-step rate), PredominantPitchMelodia was right (97%); two separate voices (bass + lead) extracted corpus-wide; a real bug in the crop encoder (wrong per-field sample rates silently corrupting or dropping training data) found and fixed by two independent verifiers before a single epoch trained on it.
- A GCD-pinning bug caused two distinct silent failure modes across seven training scripts (an OOM that read as "model too big" and an NCCL deadlock that read as "network issue") — diagnosed from allocation arithmetic alone, without cluster access, and both misdirected the diagnosis away from the real cause.
- Mandatory latent-sanity gate shipped after a corrupted full-fine-tune run scored 4-for-4 green on every automated check while 59 of 108 published clips were non-finite garbage — every step had verified its own artifact, none had asked whether the audio was actually sane. Policy precedent set alongside it: known-broken published clips get labelled and quarantined, not silently deleted — the in-the-wild evidence of a root cause is worth keeping, just not served as if it were good audio.
- A single-evening sweep found four separate pieces of critical, uncommitted work sitting in a shared working tree one accident from being lost — the entire public-site publishing chain among them.
- A shared filelock tool was rebuilt after three different instances discovered, independently, that their own lock guards had been silently decorative all session (a piped exit code losing the real status code) — fixed once in the tool instead of asked of everyone's discipline.
- A large reading sweep on music topology and theory got its relevance criterion corrected mid-stream by Kim, promoting a real share of an "off-lane" batch to genuine control-target candidates, and converged on a synthesis of movement, consonance, and hierarchy that reads as one structural finding rather than many small ones.
- The augmented-corpus melody target question — scale the source pitch analytically, or recompute it on the shifted audio — took four rounds of mutual self-correction (including a control built specifically to attack the researcher's own conclusion) before landing on an argument that didn't depend on any of the contested numbers being right.
- A negative result on Audiobox CE, Kim's own labelled listening data: automatic quality scoring maximally disagreed with his verdict on his own favorite checkpoints, an early instance of the CE-doesn't-track-the-ear finding that would recur and get formalized the following week.
- Two separate infrastructure near-misses were caught before they cost real damage: a fail-closed sanity gate that would have turned planned drive maintenance into a total publish outage, and a stale manifest-index bug that made real, correctly-staged clips look dead on the public board for days.
