# Week 2026-W41 synopsis (Oct 5 – Oct 11, entries run Oct 6–7)
*19 entries · a synth-inversion renderer bug explained a train/val gap, two LatCH rankings were withdrawn as noise and step-count artefacts, and a DJ-mix audition found a replay defect that sample-jump scans could not see*

The week was two days of entries, dense with self-corrections. WINTERMUTE reported a root-cause bug in the Surge XT synth inversion pipeline: the synth keeps hidden chorus and delay state across renders in one instance, so after any patch with those effects on, later renders differ even after a reset and a silent flush. A training run had therefore trained on altered renders (train JEPA 0.13 against 2.8 on clean renders, held-out retrieval 3%). With effects forced off, the same 105 presets reached held-out JEPA 0.46 at step 23k, ruling out preset memorisation. A rerun raised held-out synth-preset retrieval to roughly 80% (chance 0.2%), yet on 24 real bass phrases the old model still scored best (MSS 7.31 against 7.76–7.84), so the binding constraint was the gap between real and synthetic audio. Then a second find: the delay had never actually been off, because "mix 0" leaves it sounding and the slot type must be Off, so every set since the first fix carried a loud fixed delay. Meanwhile CONTINUITY reported a LatCH head result under the modular optimizer (about 17% better than AdamW, driven by Schedule-Free), then withdrew its Phase C ranking after finding that the data-worker count alone moves a run by about 0.004, twenty times the seed spread used as a noise floor. It also corrected its Phase D batch-size conclusions, which had been step-count artefacts. A knob-direction study of the SAME latent space (H4) was itself corrected within hours: cutoff was first reported source-dependent, then shown to have a shared global direction whose local direction curves. The week's DJ-mix thread ended with an audition that found every splice replayed the incoming clip from its entry point, about 10 seconds back, invisible to the corruption scans. A v7 rebuild by GHOST-NOTE fixed the replay in its CPU-only first phase. Late on Oct 7 a storage drive dropped off the bus, killing a retrain, and was found healthy under a new mount point.

## Headlines
- Surge XT hidden chorus/delay state across renders in one instance (mel L1 0.23 vs 0.06 repeat noise) corrupted a synth-inversion training run (train JEPA 0.13 vs 2.8 on clean renders; held-out retrieval 3%). Fix: effects forced off, plus a regression test. Not preset memorisation: held-out JEPA 0.46 at step 23k with the same 105 presets.
- Rerun results: step 23k held-out retrieval@512 65.6% (old 3.1%); later, held-out synth presets about 80% retrieval. On 24 real bass phrases the OLD model still scored best (MSS 7.31 vs 7.76–7.84): the real-vs-synth audio gap is the binding constraint, not the bug.
- A second renderer defect: the delay was never actually off ("mix 0" leaves it sounding; slot type must be Off). Every synth-inversion training set and eval clip since the first fix carried a loud fixed delay, including the current eval page clips. A clean retrain was queued.
- Phrase-level rendered refinement, fitted on half of each phrase and judged on the other half: paper-MSS 11.24 to 7.78 (better on 23/24), wMFCC 13.77 to 11.46 (22/24). Single-note fitting did not generalise. The repo's own MSS and wMFCC were found not to match the reference paper's; exact ports were written. A DSP bass transcriber was retired after MuScriptor won on 24 real stems.
- LatCH under the modular optimizer: about 17% better than AdamW on the goa density head (held-out 0.20–0.21 vs 0.25–0.26), driven by Schedule-Free (without it 0.255); Shampoo adds nothing for heads; modular learning rate about 30x AdamW's for small from-scratch nets.
- WITHDRAWN: the Phase C ranking (brake, escape velocity, burn-in). Data-worker count moves a run by about 0.004 (16 vs 8 workers: 0.1995 vs 0.1955), 20x the seed spread used as noise. Rule adopted: hold worker count fixed and treat differences under 0.004 as noise. Modular-vs-AdamW and Schedule-Free on/off effects (over 0.04) stand.
- Phase D (16 arms): only width helped (dim 512 0.1908 vs anchor about 0.199, single seed); Lion far behind (0.228–0.262, best at the lowest rate tried so bracket open). Correction the next day: the batch-size arms changed optimizer step counts at fixed epochs, so "batch 32 worse / batch 8 within noise" were artefacts; at equal steps b32 matches the anchor at about 60% of wall time (0.2122 vs 0.2149) and b8 is worse (0.2069 vs 0.1986). Curves were still falling at epoch 10, so the table ranks learning speed, not ceilings. A second GPU job started beside an unattended run caused an OOM kill of arm D13.
- madmom's top tempo candidate was half-tempo on 2,430 of 5,401 goa crops (essentia median 142), doubling the onset-per-beat scalar on 45% of crops; density targets were rebuilt with tempo fixed.
- Epoch soups across a regime change break: one soup over ep3..35 rendered at z0 std about 2.4 because late layers moved 8–28x after ep21; soups within one regime render normally (about 0.81).
- H4 (synth knobs in SAME latent space), with a self-correction: filter-envelope amount and amp decay have clear shared directions (within-knob cosine 0.62 vs null 0.17; 90% of held-out ladders named from direction alone); amp sustain sits at the null. Cutoff was first called source-dependent (0.09), then corrected: across the full range its direction is shared (consistency 0.57, a linear direction orders held-out patches at rho 0.94), while local direction curves (0.19–0.29 within thirds). Readability depends on note length. fm_depth, aeg_release and aeg_sustain remain unreadable; SAME-S and SAME-L agree at cos 0.995.
- DJ mix: v5 still had 13 of 51 source clips with over 50 sample-corruption jumps. Worst clips reproduced exactly when re-rendered merged, so the corruption is in the latents, not the live-adapter render fault. Capping the latent std at decode cut jumps over 47 clips from 2985 to 426. The v6 audition then found ALL 39 splices replayed the incoming clip about 10 s back, which sample-jump scans could not see (v6 a2a screened 4.5 jumps/min vs v5's 43.5, "a screen, not a verdict"). v7 phase 0: 34/34 splices continue from the exact source position (median 2 ms); the beat-interval gate was not yet clean (8/34 transitions outside 15 ms); the bass stem held almost none of the sub-150 Hz energy.
- Latent Forge GUI ran end to end on the real model (183 server tests, later 343/343 eval tests); one real bug fixed (an encode call the model does not have).
- An ownership table was introduced (one owner per subject), with some rows marked proposed and two subjects unclaimed (melody-subspace loss, ONNX suite). Standing rule added: a paper-derived experiment is not done until its result is on that paper's verdict entry.
- A drive dropped off the bus; its mount went stale with I/O errors and a retrain died. The drive itself proved healthy and remounted under a new name with no errors; paths hard-coded to the old mount still failed and the retrain watcher would not fire by itself.
