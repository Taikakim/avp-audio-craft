  Shape per field is (n_frames,) or (n_frames, 12) for HPCP, where n_frames = round(duration_sec × 100). The consumer
  (LatCHDataset in whole_track mode) slices [start_time, end_time] from these and resamples to the latent's frame count (256 for
  SA Open / SA1, variable for SA3).

  Use the bare name as --feature in train_latch.py (the dataset appends _ts for the npz lookup) — e.g. --feature
  onset_envelope_drums --target-source whole_track.
